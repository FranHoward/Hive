## High Level Description

For our final artifact, we will create a first-person 3D horror escape room game. The player wakes up trapped inside a dark room and must explore two connected rooms to find clues, interact with objects, solve tasks, and uncover the story behind the space. The goal is to escape. We plan to use scary lighting, shadows, sound effects, smoke, flames, and animated objects to create a tense environment. A bug-like creature will serve as one of the main horror elements, appearing through jump scares, background movement, and unsettling visual moments.

## Feature List

### Core Gameplay Features
- First person player movement through the scene
- Two explorable 3D rooms
- Tasks and puzzles the player must complete to escape
- Clues that reveal the story of the room
- Interactable objects that the player can pick up

### Graphics Features
- Bug creature jump scare
- Objects moving in the background
- Objects staring at the user
- Lighting and shadows
- Sound effects and background music
- Smoke particle effects
- Flame effects
- Custom bug creature model
- Textured objects and props
- Users can freely rotate the objects picked up

### Stretch Goals
- Mirrors
- More advanced creature animations
- Higher quality environmental details
